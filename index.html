AI engineering

Models, datasets, and the infrastructure under them.

Real machine-learning work, not API wrappers: models fine-tuned and evaluated properly, a dataset published for others to use, and the engineering that makes training repeatable.

Teaching a 1.5B model step-by-step math

Built a tutoring dataset from NCERT textbooks (PyMuPDF extraction) merged with Orca-Math and MetaMathQA, then QLoRA fine-tuned Qwen2.5-Math-1.5B-Instruct - 4-bit, LoRA r=32 across attention and MLP projections. GSM8K and MATH were excluded from training and used only as held-out benchmarks - a baseline-vs-finetuned eval on GSM8K and a held-out eval on MATH.

PyTorchUnslothQLoRATRL/PEFTGSM8K evals

Scraped and cleaned Yoruba Wikipedia through the MediaWiki API into an academic QA dataset, published it on Hugging Face, and fine-tuned Qwen2.5-1.5B on it - low-resource-language NLP for a language 40+ million people speak.

Open source

Plugins other builders can use.

Six Flutter plugins on pub.dev, born from production work - on-device OpenVINO inference from EdgeExl, and the AR live-streaming pipeline from Cloutt.

  • v0.1.0 openvino_genai On-device LLM inference for Flutter via Intel OpenVINO GenAI - streaming chat completion on CPU or GPU, with hardened load and fallback behaviour. pub.dev ↗
  • v0.1.0 openvino_genai_windows Its Windows x64 implementation - downloads the pinned, SHA-256-verified OpenVINO runtime, compiles the C bridge, and bundles the DLLs. pub.dev ↗
  • v0.1.7 flutter_deepar DeepAR for Flutter - camera capture, AR effect loading and switching, and a stream of processed frames for live streaming, recording, or preview. pub.dev ↗
  • v0.1.1 flutter_deepar_agora Bridges DeepAR’s processed frames into Agora RTC as an external video source - AR face filters inside live streams. pub.dev ↗
  • v0.1.2 flutter_banuba_ar The same camera-and-effects pipeline for the Banuba AR SDK. pub.dev ↗
  • v0.1.0 flutter_banuba_agora_ar And its Agora RTC bridge - Banuba-filtered frames straight into a live stream. pub.dev ↗
